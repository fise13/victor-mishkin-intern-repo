# Learning Goals

## What do I hope to learn from this internship?

I want to improve my skills and get better at reading and understanding other people's code. Working in a real codebase with a real team is the best way to learn that.

## What skills am I most excited to develop?

Frontend and mobile development. I'm most interested in the whole process of building a genuinely good product, not just writing code that works.

## What do I think I can contribute to the team?

I'm motivated and I learn quickly. I'm willing to take on tasks, ask questions when I'm stuck, and give honest feedback on the product from a fresh user's point of view, for example during my first-time experience with the app.

## What is one area I feel less confident in that I'd like to improve?

TypeScript, and software architecture: how to structure a project and make good product decisions.

## What does success in this internship look like for me?

- Better knowledge of TypeScript.
- A better understanding of architecture and how product decisions are made.
- Being comfortable reading and working in an unfamiliar codebase.
- Meeting the required hours and finishing the onboarding tasks on time (by 19 Dec 2026).
